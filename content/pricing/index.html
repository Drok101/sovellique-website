Pricing questions

Is SOVELLIQUE free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you start your shelf, keep wishlists, make a board, write in your journal, follow a starter journey and set up rituals and reminders. SOVELLIQUE Plus is an optional subscription for more room and extra features.

What is included in Plus?

Plus removes the Free limits (unlimited products, wishlists, boards and journal history) and adds premium board layouts, advanced shelf insights, additional journeys, watermark-free exports and premium personalization. See the pricing page for the full comparison.

What happens if I cancel Plus?

Plus stays on until the end of the period you have paid for. After that your account returns to Free: what you have already created stays in your account, and adding more than the Free limits allows will need Plus again.

Can I export my boards?

Yes. Boards can be exported as images to save or share. On Free, exports carry a small SOVELLIQUE mark; with Plus they are watermark-free.
