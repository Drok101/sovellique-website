One tap

Log a use in a second.

Tap Log use when you put something on. SOVELLIQUE counts it and remembers when you last used it.

  • 18 uses tells you a lipstick has earned its place.
  • Last used shows what has been sitting untouched.
  • Finished moves a product to your empties with pride.

What tracking your makeup helps with.

Shop smarter

When your shelf shows that a blush has 14 uses and a bronzer has none, your next purchase gets easier.

Use it while it is fresh

Mascara and liquid products do not last forever. Note when you opened them and replace them in good time.

Find your signatures

Your most used products tell you which shades and formulas really suit you.

Finish what you open

A project pan feels lighter when you can watch the uses add up and tick off your empties.

The best makeup bag is the one where you love everything in it.
