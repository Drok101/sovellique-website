About SOVELLIQUE

A softer way to care for your beauty, your vision and yourself.

SOVELLIQUE was made for women who love beautiful things and want a calm, personal place to keep them, and to grow.

What SOVELLIQUE is about

Five ideas shape everything in the app.

1 of 5

Organizing the things you love

Your lipstick, your favourite serum, the perfume you save for good days. When they are organized, you enjoy them more and waste less.

2 of 5

Collecting inspiration

Images, colours and words you want to keep close, gathered in one place instead of scattered across screenshots.

3 of 5

Creating a vision for your future

A clear picture of the life you want makes the next step easier to see.

4 of 5

Building gentle everyday rituals

Small things done with care, morning and evening, shape a life more than grand resolutions do.

5 of 5

Becoming the person you want to be

Not a new you. A truer you, one chapter and one page at a time.

Your beauty. Your vision. Your becoming.
The SOVELLIQUE promise
Personal, not social

A space that is only about you.

So much of the internet asks you to compare and perform. SOVELLIQUE asks for neither.

There is no public feed, no follower count and no comments. Your shelf, your boards, your journal and your goals are for you. If you want to share a board, you export it and choose where it goes.

We believe beauty is a form of self-respect, inspiration is worth collecting, and the person you are becoming deserves gentle, daily attention.
