Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know about SOVELLIQUE, Free and Plus, privacy and your account.

About SOVELLIQUE

What is SOVELLIQUE?

SOVELLIQUE is a personal beauty and lifestyle app. It brings together a digital beauty shelf, wishlists, vision boards and moodboards, daily rituals, a private journal and personal goals, so the things you love and the person you are becoming live in one calm place.

Is SOVELLIQUE free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you start your shelf, keep wishlists, make a board, write in your journal, follow a starter journey and set up rituals and reminders. SOVELLIQUE Plus is an optional subscription for more room and extra features.

What is included in Plus?

Plus removes the Free limits (unlimited products, wishlists, boards and journal history) and adds premium board layouts, advanced shelf insights, additional journeys, watermark-free exports and premium personalization. See the pricing page for the full comparison.

Is the app available on Android?

Yes. SOVELLIQUE is made for Android phones and is distributed through Google Play.

Beauty shelf and wishlists

Can I organize makeup?

Yes. Add each product with its brand, shade, category and a photo, then browse your shelf by category or search it by name or brand.

Can I track skincare products?

Yes. Skincare, haircare and fragrance live on the same shelf as makeup. You can note when you opened a product, mark it as finished and keep an eye on what needs using up soon.

Can I track product usage?

Yes. Tap Log use whenever you use a product. SOVELLIQUE counts your uses and shows when you last reached for it, and shelf insights help you see what you love and what you forget.

Can I save products to wishlists?

Yes. Save the products you want to wishlists, give each list a name, and move a product to your shelf once it is yours.

Boards, rituals, journal and goals

Can I create vision boards?

Yes. Build vision boards from your own images, words and quotes, choose a layout, and arrange the pieces the way you picture them.

Can I create moodboards?

Yes. Boards can be vision boards or moodboards, so you can collect an aesthetic, a colour story or the feel of a season alongside your bigger goals.

Can I export my boards?

Yes. Boards can be exported as images to save or share. On Free, exports carry a small SOVELLIQUE mark; with Plus they are watermark-free.

Can I create personal goals?

Yes. Set personal goals with steps, follow guided journeys one day at a time and celebrate milestones along the way.

Is the journal private?

Yes. SOVELLIQUE is a personal app, not a social network. Your journal entries are tied to your account and are not published, shared or shown to anyone else.

Account and support

What happens if I cancel Plus?

Plus stays on until the end of the period you have paid for. After that your account returns to Free: what you have already created stays in your account, and adding more than the Free limits allows will need Plus again.

How do I delete my account?

Open the My Era tab, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account, then confirm. If you subscribe to Plus, also cancel the subscription in Google Play so you are not billed again.

How can I contact support?

Visit the contact page to find the ways to reach the SOVELLIQUE team, and tell us what you need help with.
