Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

On this page

  2. Cookies on this website
  3. Cookies in the app
  4. Managing cookies

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. They can be used to remember preferences, support website functionality and understand how a website is used.

2. Cookies on this website

The SOVELLIQUE website does not set its own cookies.

The website uses Google Fonts to display its typography. When fonts are requested from Google Fonts, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser request information to deliver the font files.

The website uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. That service does not set a cookie. It records the page address, referrer, approximate location, device type, operating system, and browser. It does not tie that record to a SOVELLIQUE account. If advertising, embedded content, or other services that use cookies are added later, this policy will be updated.

3. Cookies in the SOVELLIQUE app

The SOVELLIQUE Android app does not use browser cookies.

Information you create or provide within the app, such as your beauty products, wishlists, boards, journal entries and goals, is handled separately from website cookies. Details about personal information are explained in our Privacy Policy.

4. Managing cookies

You can manage, block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Blocking cookies may affect how some websites and embedded services work.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the website's use of cookies or similar technologies changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact

For questions about this Cookie Policy, contact sovellique@gmail.com.
