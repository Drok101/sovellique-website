How to start journaling when you do not know what to write

A journal does not need to be profound. It needs to be honest and yours. Here are easy ways to begin, and to keep going.

Journaling 6 min readBy the SOVELLIQUE team
In this guide
  1. Lower the bar
  2. Try a simple format
  3. Prompts to begin with
  4. Notice how you feel
  5. Keep it private

Lower the bar

Aim for three lines, not three pages. Some days you will write more. Most days, three honest lines are enough to notice how you are doing.

Try a simple format

  • Three good things: list small moments from the day.
  • Brain dump: write whatever is on your mind for five minutes.
  • One question: answer a single prompt.
  • Photo and a line: add a picture from today and say why it mattered.

Prompts to begin with

  • What felt good today, even for a minute?
  • What am I carrying that I could put down?
  • What did I do today that I am quietly proud of?
  • What do I need more of this week?
  • What would the woman I am becoming do tomorrow?

Notice how you feel

Naming your mood (calm, tender, tired, proud) helps you see patterns over time. In SOVELLIQUE every entry can carry a mood, so you can look back at a month and see how it really felt.

Attach it to an existing habitWrite while your coffee brews or straight after brushing your teeth. Linking a new habit to an old one makes it stick.

Keep it private

Write as if no one will read it, because no one should. A private journal is where you can be unpolished. SOVELLIQUE journal entries stay in your account and are never shared or published.
