How to organize makeup so you actually use it

Most makeup collections do not need more storage. They need an edit, a simple system and a way to see what you already own. Here is a calm way to get there in an afternoon, and keep it that way.

Beauty 7 min readBy the SOVELLIQUE team
In this guide
  1. Start with a full edit
  2. Check what has expired
  3. Group products by how you use them
  4. Store it so you can see it
  5. Keep a digital record
  6. Keep it organized

Start with a full edit

Take everything out: the drawer, the travel bag, the lipstick rolling around your handbag. Put it all on a clean towel so you can see the whole collection at once. It is usually more than you think, and that is the point.

Make three piles:

  • Keep: products you have used in the last month and enjoy.
  • Question: things you like the idea of but rarely reach for.
  • Let go: anything past its best, broken, or that never suited you.

Be honest about the middle pile. If a shade is wrong or a formula annoys you, keeping it will not change that.

Check what has expired

Makeup does not last forever once opened. Look for the small open-jar symbol on the packaging with a number such as 6M or 12M: that is how many months the product is good for after opening. As a general guide:

  • Mascara and liquid eyeliner: around 3 months.
  • Liquid foundation and concealer: around 6 to 12 months.
  • Lipstick and gloss: around 12 to 18 months.
  • Powders, blush and eyeshadow: often up to 2 years.

If something smells different, has separated or has changed texture, let it go whatever the date says.

Group products by how you use them

Organizing by product type is a good start, but organizing by routine is what makes a collection easy to use. Try three zones:

  1. Everyday: the five to ten products you use most mornings. These go in the most reachable spot.
  2. Occasional: evening looks, bold lips, glitter. Close by, but not in the way.
  3. Backups: unopened products and refills, stored somewhere cool and dark.

Store it so you can see it

Clear organizers, shallow drawers with dividers and upright lipstick holders all do the same job: they let you see what you own without digging. Keep makeup away from steamy bathrooms and sunny windowsills, which shorten its life.

Wash brushes and sponges weekly, and give them their own cup so they dry upright.

Try a one-in, one-out ruleWhen a new product arrives, finish or let go of one in the same category. It keeps your collection the size you actually use.

Keep a digital record

A physical system tells you where things are. A digital one tells you what you have, what you use and what you have finished. In SOVELLIQUE, add each product to your beauty shelf with its brand, shade and category, then tap Log use when you wear it.

After a few weeks you will see your real favourites, the products that sit untouched, and exactly which nude lipstick you already own before you buy another.

Keep it organized

Set a monthly ten-minute reset: put strays back, wipe palettes, check for anything past its best and move finished products to your empties. Small, regular care beats a big clear-out every year.
