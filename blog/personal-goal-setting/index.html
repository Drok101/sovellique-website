Personal goal setting that feels kind, not pressured

Goals should pull you forward, not weigh you down. This approach keeps them meaningful, specific and small enough to actually reach.

Personal growth 7 min readBy the SOVELLIQUE team
In this guide
  1. Choose fewer goals
  2. Make each goal specific
  3. Break goals into steps
  4. Track progress you can see
  5. Review, adjust and celebrate

Choose fewer goals

Three goals you care about will take you further than ten you half remember. Look at your vision board and ask which changes would make the biggest difference this season.

Make each goal specific

"Be healthier" is a wish. "Walk for twenty minutes three times a week" is a goal. Specific goals tell you exactly what to do and when you have done it.

Break goals into steps

Write the first three steps for each goal, then focus only on the first. Saving for a trip might start with opening a separate account, then setting up a monthly transfer, then booking the flights.

Track progress you can see

Seeing progress keeps motivation alive. In SOVELLIQUE each goal shows its steps and how far you have come. Guided journeys break bigger changes into one small focus a day.

Pair every goal with a whyWrite one sentence about why each goal matters to you. On low-motivation days, the why does the work.

Review, adjust and celebrate

Check in monthly. Adjust goals that no longer fit; that is wisdom, not failure. And celebrate milestones, however small. Finishing five products or six Sunday calls to your grandmother deserves noticing.
