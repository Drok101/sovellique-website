Privacy Policy

How SOVELLIQUE collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Who we are

SOVELLIQUE is a beauty and lifestyle organization app.

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, contact sovellique@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

An account is required before you can use the app. You sign in with an email address and a password. There is no Google, Apple, or other sign-in option. Your password is used to sign in and is not saved in the app's content records. You can ask for a password reset email.

Your sign-in session is stored on your phone. On the web version of the app, it is stored in the browser.

Your profile can include a display name, a profile photo, and interests you choose from a fixed list.

Beauty information

  • Products on your shelf, including name, brand, category, photo, shade, price, currency, purchase date, opening date, expiration date, rating, notes, favorite, and status.
  • The times you log a use.
  • Wishlists and wishlist items, including name, brand, category, photo, price, currency, retailer link, notes, priority, and a savings target.

Vision and personal content

  • Vision boards and moodboards, with images, text, quotes, and a Pinterest link if you type one.
  • Goals, including a title, description, target date, and status.
  • Rituals, their steps, and the times you complete them.
  • Journal entries, including title, text, mood, prompt, date, and photos.
  • Guided-journey progress.
  • Lines you write for affirmation, gratitude, intention, and visualization, and the ones you mark as favorites.
  • The reminder times you choose.

The line shown on Home is chosen on your phone. It is not saved as its own entry.

Emails you send to sovellique@gmail.com are ordinary email. The app does not store a copy of them.

Please avoid adding sensitive personal information that is not necessary for using the app.

3. Information collected automatically

The app does not include an advertising service or a crash-reporting service. It does not save your device model, operating system version, or an advertising identifier. The Android configuration includes a microphone permission, but the app does not record audio.

It does save a short activity record on your account: your account id, an event name, and the time. The events are finishing onboarding, adding a product, adding a wishlist item, creating a board, exporting a board, completing a ritual, starting a journey, opening the Plus screen, and purchasing a subscription. That record does not include journal text, photos, notes, or prices.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent through a push service. A ritual reminder can use the ritual's title, and an expiration reminder can use the product's name. The rest of the reminder text is a fixed sentence.

The app's fonts are included in the app. Using the app does not send a request to Google for those fonts.

This website is different from the app. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, and Google receives your IP address and the request details needed to deliver the font files. The site is hosted on Vercel, which receives the connection data needed to deliver the pages, including your IP address. The site does not set its own cookies. See the Cookie Policy.

4. How information is used

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide the SOVELLIQUE app features.
  • Save and display your beauty products, wishlists and boards.
  • Store your journal entries, goals, rituals and journey progress.
  • Keep your personal content available to you.
  • Schedule the reminders you turn on.
  • Manage your SOVELLIQUE Plus subscription status.
  • Record the activity events described above.
  • Maintain and protect the app.

The legal basis for processing personal information will depend on the activity and applicable privacy law. Where required, processing may be based on providing the service you request, complying with legal obligations, your consent or legitimate interests.

5. Your personal content and privacy

SOVELLIQUE is designed as a personal space for your beauty collection, inspiration and personal growth.

The app does not have public social feeds, public profiles, followers, comments or messaging features.

Your journal entries, boards, beauty shelf, wishlists and goals are personal content. They are not intended to be published as public content or displayed to other SOVELLIQUE users.

Nothing in your journal is exported. You can choose to export a board, an affirmation card, or a journey milestone as an image. The image is offered through your phone's share sheet and can be saved to your photo library if you allow it. Exports on the free plan include the words "Made with SOVELLIQUE". You can also share a wishlist item as text: its name, brand, price, currency, retailer link, and list name. Wishlist notes are not included. Once you share or save something, the app you send it to has its own privacy practices.

6. Service providers

These services receive information so the app and website can work. The app does not use a push-notification service, an advertising service, or a crash-reporting service.

  • Supabase stores your account, the content listed above, your photos, and the activity events. Photos are kept in private storage. The app does not state which country Supabase stores this in.
  • RevenueCat receives your account id so the app can tell whether SOVELLIQUE Plus is active. The Plus expiry date and product id are then saved on your account. The app does not state which country RevenueCat stores this in. Deleting your account signs you out of RevenueCat. It does not delete RevenueCat's subscriber record.
  • Google Play handles the purchase and renewal of SOVELLIQUE Plus. Google processes payment information under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts delivers fonts for this website, as described above. The app does not request fonts from Google.
  • Vercel hosts this website and receives the connection data needed to deliver it.

7. Subscriptions and payments

SOVELLIQUE Plus subscriptions are offered through Google Play.

Payments and subscription billing are handled through Google Play under the applicable Google Play payment and subscription terms.

You can use the app on a limited free plan without paying. SOVELLIQUE Plus is a separate paid subscription and does not include a free trial. The app does not ask for your card number and does not process refunds. The price you pay for Plus is the price Google Play shows when you subscribe. The app shows a monthly plan and a yearly plan.

Google Play processes payment information under its own privacy policy.

8. Data retention and account deletion

Your content stays in your account until you delete the account or delete that item.

You can delete your account from the My Era section of the app. Deletion removes the account and the content records tied to it. It also deletes photos from your profile, shelf, wishlists, boards, and journal, up to 100 files in each of those folders. If a folder has more than 100 files, or if listing the files fails, some photos can remain.

The app does not set a further retention period. It also does not control whether Supabase, RevenueCat, or Google Play keep their own logs, backups, or billing records after you delete the account.

Deleting the account does not cancel SOVELLIQUE Plus. Cancel the subscription in Google Play if you do not want it to renew.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Request restriction of certain processing.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request a copy of information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection authority.

To exercise an applicable right or ask a privacy question, contact sovellique@gmail.com.

We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children

The app does not ask for your age and does not include an age check. It is not directed at children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at sovellique@gmail.com.

11. International data transfers

Supabase and RevenueCat process the information described above. The app does not record the country where they store it. Where the law requires a safeguard for a transfer outside your country, that provider's own safeguards apply.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SOVELLIQUE develops or when our data practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact sovellique@gmail.com.
